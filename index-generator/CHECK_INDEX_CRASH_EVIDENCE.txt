--- 4b. why a count-cell guard ALONE was not enough ---
An ordinary results table with a numeric last column. Three variants of
indexgen.py are run against it, in this order:

  BEFORE          the parent commit -- crashes, on the header row this
                  time ("count" is not an integer)
  GUARD-ONLY      the obvious minimal fix, applied here to a throwaway
                  copy of the parent commit: skip a row whose count cell
                  will not parse. No crash -- and the two DATA rows,
                  whose fifth cells ARE integers, become tools named
                  WA001 and WA002 with spurious INDEX_DRIFT findings.
  AFTER           the committed fix. Not an index table, so no rows at
                  all; the only finding is the real one about alpha.
